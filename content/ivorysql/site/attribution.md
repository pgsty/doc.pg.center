## Coverage {#coverage}

This edition includes 80 Chinese and 75 English articles. The two empty `4.7.adoc` source files are excluded. Differences between languages are preserved. Two screenshots referenced by the Chinese parameter reference are absent from the upstream repository; notices link back to the source.

## Adaptations {#adaptations}

AsciiDoc was converted to Markdown, navigation reorganized, version attributes expanded, internal links and anchors repaired, and images stored locally. Every inherited article links to its source at the fixed revision and is marked as adapted. Image bytes are preserved and deduplicated by content hash.

Historical versions, limitations, commands, performance results, and plans retain their original meaning. Older examples are not automatically recommended configurations for 5.6.
