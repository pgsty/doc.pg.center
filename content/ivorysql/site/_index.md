This site organizes the [official IvorySQL 5.6 documentation](https://docs.ivorysql.org/en/ivorysql-doc/v5.6/welcome.html) around installation, compatibility, operations, and development. Both languages come from existing upstream articles.

This is an independent documentation mirror, not the official IvorySQL website. The material remains credited to its original authors and rightsholders. Technical conditions and examples reflect the source snapshot identified on each page.
